Flying into Providence (PVD)

By car about 15 miles · 20–25 min

Pick up a rental car or rideshare at the airport. Take I-95 South, then Route 4 South toward North Kingstown. Take the Route 403 exit toward Quonset and follow it to the Marriott in Quonset.

Flying into Boston Logan (BOS)

By car about 80 miles · 1.5–2 hr

Take I-93 South through Boston to I-95 South. Continue on I-95 South past Providence, then take Route 4 South and the Route 403 exit toward Quonset to reach the Marriott.

By train about 2–2.5 hr

Take the free Silver Line SL1 bus from any Logan terminal to South Station. Board the MBTA Commuter Rail Providence Line to Wickford Junction — plan ahead and check the schedule, not every train goes all the way to Wickford. From Wickford Junction, the Marriott in Quonset is about a 15 minute drive.
